Outreach Marketing Agent
Evidence-led prospect research and marketing workflows

In development · accepting pilot and completion engagements

A multi-tenant SaaS direction combining qualified prospect discovery, personalized outreach drafting, competitor intelligence and SEO workflows.

Open product website ↗Discuss development or pilot scope ↗

Capabilities & workflows

Implemented foundations: Account/workspace flows, CRM and prospect records, campaign and draft workflows, evidence-aware research foundations, suppression/opt-out controls, tenant-scoped operations and analytics.

In-progress functionality: Prospect research and evidence enrichment, fit qualification, competitor intelligence, campaign execution workflows, provider integrations and production operations are being developed and validated. Outreach remains review-first, with human approval and suppression safeguards.

Product scope

The platform is being completed iteratively. Pilot customers can help prioritize target markets, data sources, qualification criteria, campaign workflows and provider integrations.

Technology

Multi-tenant SaaS · Python backend · TypeScript frontend · PostgreSQL

Who it can help

Small businesses, agencies and growth teams that want a research-first workflow for finding relevant prospects, preparing personalized outreach for human review and improving organic search visibility. The platform remains in development; this is not a claim of a production-ready automated outreach service.

In progress & delivery roadmap: The broader delivery vision includes richer research automation, lead scoring, competitor monitoring, personalized campaign preparation, reporting and multi-tenant growth workflows. Final modules and integrations are agreed per pilot. The product page presents current foundations alongside work that can be completed with a client. Final scope, dependencies, milestones and acceptance criteria are agreed before implementation; no unfinished integration is represented as already production-ready.
