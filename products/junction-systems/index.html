Capabilities & workflows

Core product areas: Junction POS and Junction Store; WooCommerce-based commerce; documented store components include JazzCash/EasyPaisa payment integrations, courier workflows, OTP verification and Dukaan AI chat.

In-progress / client delivery scope: Finalize the selected POS and online-store workflows, connect the client's preferred payment and courier providers, configure OTP and customer-assistance journeys, and validate the end-to-end setup for the merchant's operating model.

Product scope

Technology

Who it can help

Retailers and growing merchants looking to connect in-store POS, online-store operations, payments, delivery workflows and customer support. Junction POS and Junction Store remain product areas within Junction Systems.

In progress & delivery roadmap: Additional business-specific workflows, third-party integrations, reporting and operational automation can be scoped and completed as part of a client engagement.
