Capabilities & workflows

Documented workflows: Salon discovery by city/search; customer accounts; branch and service selection; staff-aware appointment slots; booking, cancellation and rescheduling; invoices; configurable online/offline payments; plan-gated subscriptions; shared product catalogue and orders; configured WhatsApp notifications.

In-progress / client delivery scope: Configure tenant and franchise structures, branches, services, staff schedules, subscription entitlements, payment providers, store orders and WhatsApp notification flows for the salon group's operating requirements.

Product scope

Payment gateways and WhatsApp delivery require tenant configuration; subscription entitlements control selected features. Confirm the active plan and connected providers for each deployment.

Technology

Laravel 13 · PHP 8.3+ · Blade · Vite · relational database

Who it can help

Salon owners, franchise operators, branch managers, stylists and customers who need salon discovery, appointment booking, staff scheduling, invoicing and related store workflows. Availability depends on the tenant's plan and configuration.

In progress & delivery roadmap: Client-specific salon journeys, franchise rules, additional payment or messaging providers, operational reports and workflow refinements can be completed through an agreed implementation plan. The product page presents current foundations alongside work that can be completed with a client. Final scope, dependencies, milestones and acceptance criteria are agreed before implementation; no unfinished integration is represented as already production-ready.
