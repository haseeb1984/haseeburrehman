E-commerce Audit Agent
Actionable technical audits for online storefronts

Application build · client pilots and audit-scope extensions

An audit platform that inspects public storefronts and turns technical findings into structured recommendations and reports.

Explore product / source ↗Discuss a demo or licence ↗

Capabilities & workflows

Documented audit capabilities: Browser-driven storefront inspection; technical SEO and crawlability; accessibility and performance diagnostics; conversion/UX observations; normalized findings and recommendations; customer/agency/admin workflows; license and quota checks; PDF reports and audit persistence.

In-progress / client delivery scope: Tune audit coverage for the target storefront and commerce platform, validate browser checks against representative pages, configure report branding and user roles, and complete deployment-specific quota, licensing and PDF workflows.

Product scope

Audit only websites the customer is authorized to assess. Production readiness depends on deployment, account configuration and validation against agreed storefront test cases.

Technology

Python · FastAPI · browser worker · PostgreSQL

Who it can help

E-commerce operators, digital agencies and technical teams that need evidence-led reviews of storefront SEO, accessibility, performance, crawlability and user experience, with structured findings for remediation.

In progress & delivery roadmap: Additional audit templates, scheduled re-audits, white-label agency reporting, expanded platform checks and client-specific scoring rules can be scoped as follow-on enhancements. The product page presents current foundations alongside work that can be completed with a client. Final scope, dependencies, milestones and acceptance criteria are agreed before implementation; no unfinished integration is represented as already production-ready.
