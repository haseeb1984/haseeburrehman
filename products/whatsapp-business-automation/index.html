WhatsApp Business Automation
Multi-tenant customer conversations, FAQs and bookings

Advanced implementation · production acceptance pending

A business messaging platform built around the official Meta WhatsApp Business Cloud API, with tenant-aware operations and controlled automation.

Explore product / source ↗Discuss a demo or licence ↗

Capabilities & workflows

Multi-tenant workspaces and role authorization; business profiles, services, staff and schedules; official Meta Cloud API connection; signed/idempotent webhooks; encrypted messages/credentials; approved templates; staff inbox and handover; FAQ assistant; booking, Google Calendar OAuth/FreeBusy and event reconciliation.

Product scope

Actual Neon connectivity and migration rehearsal, Meta/Google credential acceptance, Hostinger runtime/cron validation, security/load tests, backup restore and operational checks remain required before production readiness.

Technology

Laravel 13 · React 19 · TypeScript · Inertia · Meta Cloud API · Google Calendar

Who it can help

Appointment-led businesses and customer-service teams that manage WhatsApp enquiries, FAQs, staff handover and booking conversations. Production use depends on verified Meta/Google credentials, infrastructure acceptance and operational testing.
